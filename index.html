A Little About Us

At Rainbow Photography, we are passionate about capturing the essence of life through our lenses. Founded by Amelie Coetzee, our studio specializes in vibrant landscapes, heartfelt portraits, and unforgettable event photography.

Our mission is simple: to turn fleeting moments into lasting memories. We believe every photograph tells a story, and we strive to create a comfortable and enjoyable experience for our clients, allowing their true selves to shine through.
